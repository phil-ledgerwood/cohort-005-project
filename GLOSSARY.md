# Cadence

An online course platform where learners buy courses, enroll in them, and work through their lessons.

## Language

### Buying and access

**Purchase**:
A payment by one buyer for access to a course, either for themselves or as a team licence.

**Enrollment**:
One learner's access to one course, granted by a Purchase.

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A learner's ask to get their money back for a Purchase, which staff either approve or decline.
_Avoid_: Cancellation, Chargeback, Reversal, Refund (for the ask itself)

**Refund**:
Money returned to a learner against a Purchase, issued only when a Refund Request is approved; it ends every Enrollment that Purchase granted.
_Avoid_: Cancellation, Chargeback, Reversal
